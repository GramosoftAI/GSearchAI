## Table: `employee_employment` (DRAFT)
**Purpose:** The 'employee_employment' table stores detailed employment information for each employee, including their job title, department, employment type, and other related details. It serves as a central record linking employees to their specific roles and organizational structure.

**Synonyms:** employment_details, employee_job_info

**Caveats & Filtering Rules:** All columns are non-nullable, ensuring complete records. The 'employee_id' column references the 'employees' table, maintaining referential integrity.

**Relationships:** references employees

## Table: `employees` (DRAFT)
**Purpose:** The 'employees' table stores detailed information about individual employees within the organization, serving as a central repository for personal and identification data. It is referenced by the 'employee_employment' table to link employment details to specific employees.

**Synonyms:** staff, personnel

**Caveats & Filtering Rules:** All columns are non-nullable, ensuring complete records for each employee. The 'id' column is the primary key and is unique across all rows.

**Relationships:** referenced by employee_employment
